About the data

Fields
The newest edition of each collection in the Fields of the World harmonized field data, read from its single PMTiles archive. Only the collections an open height model covers are drawn; zoomed out, the map shades how much of the land those fields cover instead (precomputed per 600 m cell). In some (Germany's states, Luxembourg) a field is a block that several crops can share. Outlines are those the farmers or the authorities declared, and can be off by a few metres.
Heights
Open terrain models of 1 m or finer, only laser points on the ground, read from each publisher's own service while you watch:
    Fields over 600 m across are read at a coarser cell size, to keep the download small.
    Colours
    Each field gets its own scale: the lowest 2 % of its cells are dark blue, the highest 2 % yellow, and a light from the north-west adds shading. Two fields in the same colours can differ by metres; read the heights under the histogram, or hover over the field.
    Caveats
    Fields and heights are from different years: a field that was levelled, dug or built on since the flight does not match. Heights are above each country's own sea level, so they do not compare across borders.
    Built with
    OpenLayers, ol-pmtiles and geotiff.js; everything is computed in your browser. Code on GitHub.
