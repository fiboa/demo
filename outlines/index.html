About the data

Fields
The collections of the Fields of the World harmonized field data, each read from its own PMTiles file of its newest edition. Each collection states how its outlines were made in its boundaries property: declared (by farmers or their paying agency), mapped (drawn by hand from imagery) or inferred (derived by a model). The figures count the fields of the newest edition of each collection, without the collections of field blocks.
