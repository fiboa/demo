No Dutch field is flat

Click a field. This page fetches its outline from the parcel register and every half metre of its height from AHN4, the laser-measured height map of the Netherlands, and stretches the colours over that one field. Differences of a few centimetres show up: old creek beds, ridges, hollows, the camber of a field drained towards its ditches.

About the data

Fields
BRP Gewaspercelen 2025 by RVO, the register of agricultural parcels, through the PDOK OGC API (public domain). The outline and the crop are the ones declared for 15 May 2025.
Heights
AHN4, Actueel Hoogtebestand Nederland, through the PDOK WCS (CC0): 0.5 m cells, flown between 2020 and 2022, heights in metres above NAP. Ground is the terrain model (DTM): only laser points on the ground, so buildings, water and dense trees leave gaps. Surface (DSM) includes whatever stood on the field when it was flown, such as crops, hedges and trees; on a field that was bare then, the two are the same. Fields over 600 m across are read at a coarser cell size, to keep the download small.
Colours
Each field gets its own scale: the lowest 2 % of its cells are dark blue, the highest 2 % yellow, and a light from the north-west adds shading. Two fields in the same colours can differ by metres; read the heights under the histogram, or hover over the field.
Caveats
The fields are from 2025, the heights from 2020–2022: a field that was levelled, dug or built on since does not match. Outlines in the register are drawn by the farmers and can be off by a few metres.
Built with
OpenLayers and geotiff.js; everything is computed in your browser. Code on GitHub.
