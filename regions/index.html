Farming regions

Some landscapes are named after what grows in them: the Corn Belt, the wine country of Napa and Bordeaux, the sea of olive trees around Jaén. Colour the fields of one crop and the region draws itself, from a thousand kilometres across down to the single field.

    About the data

    Fields
    The newest edition of every collection in the Fields of the World harmonized field data with a crop, mapped to the HCAT crop codes, which this page sorts into groups. Collections without crops show as fields without colour.
    Zoomed out
    Further out than a few kilometres per field, the map shows an overview: the land in five-sided A5 cells of equal area, from 133,000 km² to 700 m across, coloured by the share of the cell covered by fields of the chosen crops. Every field counts once, in the cell of its centre: collections that repeat another (field blocks, EuroCrops copies of a national register, the Spanish regions within Spain's own register) are left out.
    Coverage
    Only countries and regions that publish their fields are on the map: most of Europe and the United States (the USDA crop sequence boundaries), not yet South America, Africa or most of Asia.
    Built with
    DuckDB with the A5 extension sums the fields per cell, tippecanoe makes the overview PMTiles; OpenLayers and ol-pmtiles draw it and the fields. Code on GitHub.
